Akwaaba Racing – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Applies to the Akwaaba Racing game for Android (Google Play)

Who we are

Akwaaba Racing is developed and published by Akwaaba Racing (Ghana). Contact: akwaabaracing@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. Your game progress is stored only on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games, we keep a purchase account so your purchases can be restored on another phone.
  • If you buy something, we check the purchase with Google Play and keep a record of the order.
  • If you choose to watch a rewarded video, Google AdMob shows the ad and confirms it to us so we can give you the reward.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy

Your game progress, settings, garage and Coins are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rewarded videos are optional. They are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are used, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), ad interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in the game (Shop → Ad privacy choices). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who receives data

  • Google Play (payments and purchase verification) and Google Play Games Services (sign-in).
  • Google AdMob (rewarded videos).
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Firestore in the europe-west1 region, Belgium), which hosts our purchase server.

These companies process data for us or as independent services under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Purchase account: until you delete it.
  • Order records: as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention rules require (up to 7 years). After an account is deleted they no longer contain your player ID.
  • Rewarded-video records: 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without signing in, buying or watching ads – the whole game can be played this way.
  • Delete your purchase account at any time: how to delete your account.
  • Ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it: email akwaabaracing@gmail.com.

Children

Akwaaba Racing is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Purchase receipts are digitally signed so they cannot be forged, and the server's keys are kept in Google Cloud Secret Manager.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
