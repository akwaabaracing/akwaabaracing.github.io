Akwaaba Racing

Developer: Akwaaba Racing · Request deletion of your account and its data

Akwaaba Racing does not have usernames or passwords. When you sign in with Google Play Games, our server keeps a purchase account for you, so that your purchases can be restored on another phone. You can delete it at any time.

How to delete your account

Option 1 – in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Akwaaba Racing and make sure you are signed in to Google Play Games.
  2. Go to Settings → Game and scroll down to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my purchase account, then tap it again to confirm.

Option 2 – by email (if you can no longer open the game)

  1. Email akwaabaracing@gmail.com with the subject Akwaaba Racing account deletion.
  2. Include the order number (it starts with GPA.) of any purchase you made in the game. You can find it in the purchase receipt email from Google Play or under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history in the Play Store. We use it to find your account, because we do not store your name or email address.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Google Play Games player ID and the purchase account linked to it.
  • The list of purchases linked to the account, purchased Diamonds spent, and its spending history.
  • The link between your Google Play orders and you.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Order records (order number, product, date, price status and refund status), without any player ID. These are kept for as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention rules require (up to 7 years).
  • Rewarded-video records contain only a random number for the installation, not your identity, and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
Please note: after deletion, purchases already on your phone stay there, but they can no longer be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling. Your game progress is stored only on your phone and is not affected. Signing in again later starts a new, empty purchase account.
