Nine cars. Five motorcycles.

From the Kwaku 1.6 Saloon and the Tro-Tro Express to rally cars, GTs, the Nyame X Hypercar and the Black Star Sovereign – plus motorcycles from the Okada 150 Street to the Volta GP Prototype. Every vehicle has its own engine sound, gearbox and handling.

  • Paints, finishes, liveries and number plates
  • Rims, horns, victory music and rider gear
  • Earn Coins by racing to unlock vehicles and upgrades
  • Season Pass with free and premium rewards

Fair to play

Everything that affects racing is earned by playing. Optional purchases (Diamonds and the Supporter Edition) are cosmetic or convenience items. Sign in with Google Play Games to restore purchases on a new phone. An optional short video gives bonus Coins, up to five a day. Your progress is stored on your phone.
